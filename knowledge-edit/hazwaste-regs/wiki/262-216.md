§text An eligible academic entity that generates hazardous waste outside of a laboratory is not eligible to manage that hazardous waste under this subpart; and
§a (a) Remains subject to the generator requirements of §[[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-11]] and 262.15 for large quantity generators and small quantity generators (if the hazardous waste is managed in a satellite accumulation area), and all other applicable generator requirements of 40 CFR part 262, with respect to that hazardous waste; or
§b (b) Remains subject to the conditional exemption of [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-14]] for very small quantity generators, with respect to that hazardous waste.
