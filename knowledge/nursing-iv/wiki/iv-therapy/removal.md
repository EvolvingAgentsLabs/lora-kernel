title: Removing a peripheral catheter
when: A catheter is coming out and you need the principles, not the steps.
what: Direction of pull, integrity, and bleeding.
parent: nursing-iv/wiki/iv-therapy
children: [nursing-iv/wiki/iv-therapy/removal/pressure-after-removal]
source: lora-kernel, restating Open RN ch. 23 (CC BY 4.0) — a study card, not clinical guidance
---
Withdraw parallel to the skin, slowly and steadily, under gauze. Inspect the catheter to confirm it is intact. Control bleeding with pressure before dressing.
