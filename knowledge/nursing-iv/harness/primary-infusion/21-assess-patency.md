title: Assess patency with a saline flush
when: The catheter cap is clean and dry and you hold a prefilled saline syringe.
what: The turbulent stop-start saline flush that shows an IV site is patent.

Assess IV site patency: attach a prefilled normal saline syringe purged of air, undo the clamp, and inject {{flush_ml}} mL of normal saline using a turbulent stop-start technique. If resistance is felt, do not force the flush.
