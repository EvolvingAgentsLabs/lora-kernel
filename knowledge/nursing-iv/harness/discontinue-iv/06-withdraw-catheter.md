kind: step
title: Withdraw the catheter
when: The dressing is loose and the catheter is still in the vein.
what: Pulling the catheter out parallel to the skin under a gauze pad.
requires: [nursing-iv/harness/discontinue-iv/05-loosen-dressing, nursing-iv/harness/discontinue-iv/04-clamp-iv]
next: nursing-iv/harness/discontinue-iv/07-hold-pressure

source: Nursing Skills (Open RN), ch. 23 IV Therapy Management, NBK596734 — CC BY 4.0
---
Place a gauze pad over the IV site and gently pull the IV out parallel to the skin in a slow and steady motion.
