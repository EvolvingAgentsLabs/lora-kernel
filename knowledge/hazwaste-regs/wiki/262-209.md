§a (a) Large quantity generators and small quantity generators—an eligible academic entity must ensure that a trained professional makes a hazardous waste determination, pursuant to [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-11]], for unwanted material in any of the following areas:
§a-1 (1) In the laboratory before the unwanted material is removed from the laboratory, in accordance with [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-210]];
§a-2 (2) Within 4 calendar days of arriving at an on-site central accumulation area, in accordance with [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-211]]; and
§a-3 (3) Within 4 calendar days of arriving at an on-site interim status or permitted treatment, storage or disposal facility, in accordance with [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-212]].
§b (b) Very small quantity generators. An eligible academic entity must ensure that a trained professional makes a hazardous waste determination, pursuant to [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-11]](a) through (d), for unwanted material in the laboratory before the unwanted material is removed from the laboratory, in accordance with [[hazwaste-regs/wiki/262-210]].
