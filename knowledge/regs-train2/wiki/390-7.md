§a (a) In part 325 of subchapter A and in this subchapter, unless the context requires otherwise:
§a-1 (1) Words imparting the singular include the plural;
§a-2 (2) Words imparting the plural include the singular;
§a-3 (3) Words imparting the present tense include the future tense.
§b (b) In this subchapter the word—
§b-1 (1) Officer includes any person authorized by law to perform the duties of the office;
§b-2 (2) Shall is used in an imperative sense;
§b-3 (3) Must is used in an imperative sense;
§b-4 (4) Should is used in a recommendatory sense;
§b-5 (5) May is used in a permissive sense; and
§b-6 (6) Includes is used as a word of inclusion, not limitation.
