§a (a) The rules in this part apply to each motor carrier engaged in the transportation of hazardous materials by a motor vehicle which must be marked or placarded in accordance with § 177.823 of this title and to—
§a-1 (1) Each officer or employee of the motor carrier who performs supervisory duties related to the transportation of hazardous materials; and
§a-2 (2) Each person who operates or who is in charge of a motor vehicle containing hazardous materials.
§b (b) Each person designated in paragraph (a) of this section must know and obey the rules in this part.
