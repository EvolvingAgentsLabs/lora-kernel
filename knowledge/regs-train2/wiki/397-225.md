§text A party to a proceeding under [[regs-train2/wiki/397-205]](a), [[regs-train2/wiki/397-213]](a), or [[regs-train2/wiki/397-223]](a) may seek review by the appropriate district court of the United States of the decision of the Administrator under such proceeding only by filing a petition with such court within 60 days after the final agency decision.
