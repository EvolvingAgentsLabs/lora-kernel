§text An applicant for medical examiner certification must complete a training program that:
§a (a) Is conducted by a training provider that:
§a-1 (1) Is accredited by a nationally recognized medical profession accrediting organization to provide continuing education units; and
§a-2 (2) Meets the following administrative requirements:
§a-2-i (i) Provides training participants with proof of participation.
§a-2-ii (ii) Provides FMCSA point of contact information to training participants.
§b (b) Provides training to medical examiners on the following topics:
§b-1 (1) Background, rationale, mission, and goals of the FMCSA medical examiner's role in reducing crashes, injuries, and fatalities involving commercial motor vehicles.
§b-2 (2) Familiarization with the responsibilities and work environment of commercial motor vehicle operation.
§b-3 (3) Identification of the operator and obtaining, reviewing, and documenting operator medical history, including prescription and over-the-counter medications.
§b-4 (4) Performing, reviewing, and documenting the operator's medical examination.
§b-5 (5) Performing, obtaining, and documenting additional diagnostic tests or medical opinion from a medical specialist or treating physician.
§b-6 (6) Informing and educating the operator about medications and non-disqualifying medical conditions that require remedial care.
§b-7 (7) Determining operator certification outcome and period for which certification should be valid.
§b-8 (8) FMCSA reporting and documentation requirements.
§text-2 Guidance on the core curriculum specifications for use by training providers is available from FMCSA.
