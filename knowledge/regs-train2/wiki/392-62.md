§text No person shall drive a bus and a motor carrier shall not require or permit a person to drive a bus unless—
§a (a) All standees on the bus are rearward of the standee line or other means prescribed in [[regs-train2/wiki/393-90]] of this subchapter;
§b (b) All aisle seats in the bus conform to the requirements of [[regs-train2/wiki/393-91]] of this subchapter; and
§c (c) Baggage or freight on the bus is stowed and secured in a manner which assures—
§c-1 (1) Unrestricted freedom of movement to the driver and his proper operation of the bus;
§c-2 (2) Unobstructed access to all exits by any occupant of the bus; and
§c-3 (3) Protection of occupants of the bus against injury resulting from the falling or displacement of articles transported in the bus.
