§text To receive medical examiner certification from FMCSA under §[[regs-train2/wiki/390-123]] through 390.135, a qualified VA examiner must pass the medical examiner certification test developed and provided by FMCSA and administered through a web-based system operated by the Department of Veterans Affairs.
