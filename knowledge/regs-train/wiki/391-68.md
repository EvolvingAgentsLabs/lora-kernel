§text The following rules in this part do not apply to a private motor carrier of passengers (nonbusiness) and its drivers:
§a (a) Section 391.11(b)(1) and (7) (relating to general qualifications of drivers);
§b (b) Subpart C (relating to disclosure of, investigation into, and inquiries about the background, character, and driving record of, drivers);
§c (c) So much of §[[regs-train/wiki/391-41]] and 391.45 as require a driver to be medically examined and to have a medical examiner's certificate on his/her person; and
§d (d) Subpart F (relating to maintenance of files and records).
