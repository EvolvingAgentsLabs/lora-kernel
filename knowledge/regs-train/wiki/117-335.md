§a (a) Any written assurance required by this part must contain the following elements:
§a-1 (1) Effective date;
§a-2 (2) Printed names and signatures of authorized officials;
§a-3 (3) The applicable assurance under:
§a-3-i (i) Section 117.136(a)(2);
§a-3-ii (ii) Section 117.136(a)(3);
§a-3-iii (iii) Section 117.136(a)(4);
§a-3-iv (iv) Section 117.430(c)(2);
§a-3-v (v) Section 117.430(d)(2); or
§a-3-vi (vi) Section 117.430(e)(2);
§b (b) A written assurance required under [[regs-train/wiki/117-136]](a)(2), (3), or (4) must include:
§b-1 (1) Acknowledgement that the facility that provides the written assurance assumes legal responsibility to act consistently with the assurance and document its actions taken to satisfy the written assurance; and
§b-2 (2) Provision that if the assurance is terminated in writing by either entity, responsibility for compliance with the applicable provisions of this part reverts to the manufacturer/processor as of the date of termination.
