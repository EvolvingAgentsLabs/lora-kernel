§a (a) FDA may withdraw a qualified facility exemption under [[regs-train/wiki/117-5]](a):
§a-1 (1) In the event of an active investigation of a foodborne illness outbreak that is directly linked to the qualified facility; or
§a-2 (2) If FDA determines that it is necessary to protect the public health and prevent or mitigate a foodborne illness outbreak based on conditions or conduct associated with the qualified facility that are material to the safety of the food manufactured, processed, packed, or held at such facility.
§b (b) Before FDA issues an order to withdraw a qualified facility exemption, FDA:
§b-1 (1) May consider one or more other actions to protect the public health or mitigate a foodborne illness outbreak, including a warning letter, recall, administrative detention, suspension of registration, refusal of food offered for import, seizure, and injunction;
§b-2 (2) Must notify the owner, operator, or agent in charge of the facility, in writing, of circumstances that may lead FDA to withdraw the exemption, and provide an opportunity for the owner, operator, or agent in charge of the facility to respond in writing, within 15 calendar days of the date of receipt of the notification, to FDA's notification; and
§b-3 (3) Must consider the actions taken by the facility to address the circumstances that may lead FDA to withdraw the exemption.
