§text An order to withdraw a qualified facility exemption under [[regs-train/wiki/117-5]](a) must include the following information:
§a (a) The date of the order;
§b (b) The name, address, and location of the qualified facility;
§c (c) A brief, general statement of the reasons for the order, including information relevant to one or both of the following circumstances that leads FDA to issue the order:
§c-1 (1) An active investigation of a foodborne illness outbreak that is directly linked to the facility; or
§c-2 (2) Conditions or conduct associated with a qualified facility that are material to the safety of the food manufactured, processed, packed, or held at such facility.
§d (d) A statement that the facility must either:
§d-1 (1) Comply with subparts C and G of this part on the date that is 120 calendar days after the date of receipt of the order, or within a reasonable timeframe, agreed to by FDA, based on a written justification, submitted to FDA, for a timeframe that exceeds 120 calendar days from the date of receipt of the order; or
§d-2 (2) Appeal the order within 15 calendar days of the date of receipt of the order in accordance with the requirements of [[regs-train/wiki/117-264]].
§e (e) A statement that a facility may request that FDA reinstate an exemption that was withdrawn by following the procedures in [[regs-train/wiki/117-287]];
§f (f) The text of section 418(l) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and of this subpart;
§g (g) A statement that any informal hearing on an appeal of the order must be conducted as a regulatory hearing under part 16 of this chapter, with certain exceptions described in [[regs-train/wiki/117-270]];
§h (h) The mailing address, telephone number, email address, fax number, and name of the FDA Division Director in whose division the facility is located (or, in the case of a foreign facility, the same information for the Director of the Office of Compliance in the Center for Food Safety and Applied Nutrition); and
§i (i) The name and the title of the FDA representative who approved the order.
