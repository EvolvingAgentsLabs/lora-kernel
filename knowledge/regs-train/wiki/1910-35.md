§text OSHA will deem an employer demonstrating compliance with the exit-route provisions of NFPA 101, Life Safety Code, 2009 edition, or the exit-route provisions of the International Fire Code, 2009 edition, to be in compliance with the corresponding requirements in §[[regs-train/wiki/1910-34]], 1910.36, and 1910.37 (incorporated by reference, see section § 1910.6).
