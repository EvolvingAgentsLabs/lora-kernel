§text The provisions of [[regs-train/wiki/391-21]] (relating to applications for employment), [[regs-train/wiki/391-23]] (relating to investigations and inquiries), and [[regs-train/wiki/391-31]] (relating to road tests) do not apply to a driver who was a single-employer driver (as defined in § 390.5 of this subchapter) of a private motor carrier of passengers (business) as of July 1, 1994, so long as the driver continues to be a single-employer driver of that motor carrier.
