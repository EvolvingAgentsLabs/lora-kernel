§a (a) Requirement for a food safety plan. (1) You must prepare, or have prepared, and implement a written food safety plan.
§a-2 (2) The food safety plan must be prepared, or its preparation overseen, by one or more preventive controls qualified individuals.
§b (b) Contents of a food safety plan. The written food safety plan must include:
§b-1 (1) The written hazard analysis as required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-130]](a)(2);
§b-2 (2) The written preventive controls as required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-135]](b);
§b-3 (3) The written supply-chain program as required by subpart G of this part;
§b-4 (4) The written recall plan as required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-139]](a); and
§b-5 (5) The written procedures for monitoring the implementation of the preventive controls as required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-145]](a);
§b-6 (6) The written corrective action procedures as required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-150]](a)(1); and
§b-7 (7) The written verification procedures as required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-165]](b).
§c (c) Records. The food safety plan required by this section is a record that is subject to the requirements of subpart F of this part.
