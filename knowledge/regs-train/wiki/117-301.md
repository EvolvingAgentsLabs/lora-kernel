§a (a) Except as provided by paragraphs (b) and (c) of this section, all records required by this part are subject to all requirements of this subpart.
§b (b) The requirements of [[regs-train/wiki/117-310]] apply only to the written food safety plan.
§c (c) The requirements of [[regs-train/wiki/117-305]](b), (d), (e), and (f) do not apply to the records required by [[regs-train/wiki/117-201]].
