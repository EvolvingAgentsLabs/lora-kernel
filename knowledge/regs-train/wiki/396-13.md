§text Before driving a motor vehicle, the driver shall:
§a (a) Be satisfied that the motor vehicle is in safe operating condition;
§b (b) Review the last driver vehicle inspection report if required by [[regs-train/wiki/396-11]](a)(2)(i); and
§c (c) Sign the report to acknowledge that the driver has reviewed it and that there is a certification that the required repairs have been performed. The signature requirement does not apply to listed defects on a towed unit which is no longer part of the vehicle combination.
