§text We will waive any requirement of this subpart with respect to any class of persons, vehicles, food, or nonfood products, when we determine that:
§a (a) The waiver will not result in the transportation of food under conditions that would be unsafe for human or animal health; and
§b (b) The waiver will not be contrary to the public interest.
