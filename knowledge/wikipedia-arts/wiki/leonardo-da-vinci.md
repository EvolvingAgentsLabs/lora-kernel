Leonardo di ser Piero da Vinci (1452-1519) was an Italian polymath active as a painter, sculptor, architect, engineer and scientist. His paintings include the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper. Besides paintings, he left more drawings than any other Renaissance artist, filed under `drawings` in this wiki.
